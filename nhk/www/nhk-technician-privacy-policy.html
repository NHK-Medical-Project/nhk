Privacy Policy – NHK Technician

Effective date: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NHK Medical Pvt Ltd ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the NHK Technician mobile application (the "App"; Android package com.threesixty.nhktechnician, iOS bundle ID threesixty.nhk).

The App is an internal work tool for field technicians employed by or contracted to NHK Medical Pvt Ltd. It is not intended for the general public. Accounts are created by your employer; you cannot sign up in the App.

2. Device permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate) – for duty and job check-in as described above. Required to go on duty.
  • Camera – to take photos for job attachments, only when you tap to do so.
  • Photos / files – to pick an image or document to attach, only when you choose one.
  • Notifications – to alert you to job assignments and updates.
  • Internet / network state – to communicate with our servers and detect when you are offline.

You can revoke any permission in your device settings; some features will then stop working.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and provide the App's features.
  • To assign, track and verify field jobs and attendance.
  • To record payments collected and calculate technician payouts.
  • To send operational push notifications.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse (for example, detecting simulated locations).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Sharing and service providers

  • Our business systems – data is stored on our ERP servers, operated for us by our technology partner 360ithub.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging – to deliver push notifications. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Authorised staff – supervisors and office staff of NHK Medical Pvt Ltd can see your work records.
  • Legal – where required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety.

5. Data security

Data is sent to our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to server data is limited to authorised personnel. Session information on the device is held in the App's private storage and is removed when you sign out or uninstall the App. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Data retention

We keep work, attendance, location and payment records for as long as you work with us and afterwards for as long as needed for accounting, tax, legal and audit purposes, after which they are deleted or anonymised. Push tokens are removed when you sign out or when they expire.

7. Your rights and account deletion

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to have your account closed, by emailing nhkmedical@gmail.com or contacting your supervisor. We will respond within 30 days. Some records (for example, payment and payroll records) may need to be kept to meet legal obligations, even after your account is deleted.

8. Children

The App is intended only for adult employees and contractors. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version at this address and change the effective date above.
